Community Guidelines

KAMUPUS is a place for students to connect, share and support each other. To keep it safe for everyone, we ask that you follow these rules. Breaking them can get your account warned, restricted, or removed.

1. Be respectful

No insults, harassment, threats, hate speech, or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or appearance. Disagree with ideas — not with people.

2. No bullying

Repeatedly targeting someone, mocking them, spreading rumours, or sharing embarrassing content about them is bullying. It is not allowed in posts, comments, chats, or videos. Anonymous posts are still traceable to your account.

3. Protect privacy

Do not share anyone's personal information — phone number, address, screenshots of private chats, or photos — without their permission. This includes "exposing" someone in a post.

4. Keep content appropriate

No sexual content, graphic violence, illegal activity, or content that promotes self-harm. No spam, scams, or fake listings in the marketplace.

5. Report, don't retaliate

If someone bullies you or someone else, use the Report button. Do not fight back with the same behaviour — that makes things worse and you may be penalised too.

6. Consequences

  • First warning: your content may be removed and you'll get a notice.
  • Repeat offence: temporary restriction from posting or chatting.
  • Serious or repeated bullying: account suspension and referral to your school.
  • Illegal content: reported to the relevant authorities.

7. Your safety tools

You can block any user from your chat — they won't be able to message you or see your posts. You can report any message, post, comment, or video. Reports go to the admin team and are reviewed within 24 hours.

Last updated: November 2026.
Questions? Contact your school administrator.
